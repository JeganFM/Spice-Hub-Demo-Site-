The Chef

From Kafuta to ten kitchens, back home again.

Saikou "Chef" Bojang was born in Kafuta and raised in Brikama, on the south bank of The Gambia. His path into cooking started almost by accident — working hotel reception in England, he stepped in one day when the breakfast chef called in sick, and never really left the kitchen again. From there he went on to work as an executive chef across ten different countries.

In June 2022 he brought that experience home and opened Spice Hub — part restaurant, part training ground, with around five tables and a kitchen built to teach as much as it cooks. Ten young Gambian cooks have already trained here and gone on to hotels, restaurants and cruise ship kitchens of their own.

A small touch regulars mention: every table keeps a deck of cards on it, so waiting for a dish that's cooked to order never feels like waiting.
